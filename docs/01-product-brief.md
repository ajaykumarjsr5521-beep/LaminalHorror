# 01 — Product Brief & Creative Direction

Status: DRAFT (awaiting approval)

## Working title
**Nocturne Annex** (working; clearance search required before store listing)

## One-line pitch
A first-person psychological horror-exploration game set in a closed-for-the-night civic annex whose corridors rearrange when you aren't looking — you are the overnight archivist trying to find out why the building kept a file on you.

## Pillars
1. **Dread over gore.** Tension from absence, sound, light, and wrongness in familiar spaces. No gore focus, no cheap jump-scare reliance.
2. **Fair mystery.** Every clue is findable, every puzzle solvable by observation. No pixel-hunts.
3. **Liminal places.** Fluorescent offices, waiting rooms, stairwells, loading docks — empty, too quiet, subtly incorrect.
4. **Respect the player.** Generous checkpoints, pauseable at any time, no manipulative retention.

## Original world (seed)
- **Setting:** The Halloran-Vey Records Annex ("the Annex"), a fictional municipal archive in an unnamed, vaguely 1990s city. Night shift. The building is larger inside than its blueprints allow.
- **Protagonist:** Imre Calloway, a temp archivist (voice-less; thoughts via notes/captions). Hired through an agency that no longer answers calls.
- **Premise:** Every file in the Annex is a person's unrecorded moment. Imre finds file **C-0** — his own — dated tomorrow.
- **Entity (optional for MVP):** **The Indexer** — a tall, faceless figure in a clerk's cardigan that "files" anything it finds out of place. Behaviour: patrols by sound/light rules; never speaks. Original design; see GDD §7.
- **Terminology:** "Shelving" (the shifting corridors), "Misfile" (a wrong object/room), "Cross-reference" (a clue linking two places), "Stamp" (an environmental puzzle key).
- **Tone refs (abstract, not to copy):** institutional dread, analogue technology, hum of machinery, bureaucratic uncanny.

## Visual identity
Desaturated sodium-yellow and cold green fluorescent palette; a single warm accent (amber desk lamps = safety). Slight film-grain and CRT-ish UI. Low-poly with strong baked lighting; texture resolution kept small (≤1024) for mobile.

## Audio identity
Room tone beds, HVAC hum, distant copier thunk, rubber-soled footsteps, muffled PA announcements. Silence used deliberately. Music minimal, drone-based, original or CC0.

## Audience & rating intent
Teens/adults. Final rating is set by the IARC questionnaire answers in Play Console; we design to avoid graphic violence, real-world gambling, and user-generated content.

## Return-appeal (non-manipulative)
- Optional lore (cross-references) and a "Complete the Index" collection.
- Two endings determined by choices, plus a short **Shuffle Mode** (re-ordered clue/item placement for replay) post-MVP.
- No daily rewards, streaks, push notifications, or timers.

## Out of scope for MVP
Multiple levels, multiplayer, accounts, cloud save, ads, IAP, procedural levels, VR.
